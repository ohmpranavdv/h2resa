About PSCGT
PSG College of Technology (PSG CT), founded in 1951 by PSG & Sons’ Charities, stands as an eminent institution in the field of education. It is an ISO 9001-2015 certified autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University, Chennai. The college offers a diverse array of academic programs with 21 undergraduate and 24 postgraduate programs covering Engineering and Technology, Computer Applications, Management Sciences, Basic and Applied Sciences. Distinguished by its commitment to innovation and research, PSG CT houses several cutting-edge Centers of Excellence funded by MHRD, DST and other agencies. TIFAC Core, Advanced Machine Tool Research Centre, Virtual Instrumentation Centre, Centre of Excellence for Industrial and Home Textiles, Centre for Nano-technology, Centre for Robotics, Centre for Excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Software, Danfoss Centre of Excellence in Climate and Energy, and Centre of Excellence for Welding Engineering and Technology are a few to mention. In addition to its academic pursuits, PSG CT has a strong industrial presence. The PSG Industrial Institute operates manufacturing units for castings, machine tools, pumps, motors and technical textiles. PSG CT's collaboration with industry, research institutes, alumni, and entrepreneurs underscores its commitment to holistic education, innovation, and industry relevance.

About Department of Automobile Engineering

The department was established with an undergraduate programme in Automobile engineering in 1999.

The faculty of the department has published 200+ technical papers in various National and International conferences and journals from 1999. The department is well equipped with laboratories like PACE Lab, Vehicle Servicing Lab, PSG Motorsports Lab, Electric Hybrid Vehicle Lab, Engine Trouble Shooting Lab, Vehicle Performance and Characteristic Lab, Two & Three Wheeler Lab, Automotive Steering System Lab, Automotive Simulation Lab, Automotive Electronics Lab, CAD & CAE Lab, Fuel Cell Lab, Centre for Automotive Research and Testing Lab & Centre of Excellence - Industrial & Home Textiles lab. The software packages available in the department include Pro-E, Solidworks, Solid edge, NX, Siemens, CATIA, Unigraphics, ANSYS, ADAMS, LSDYNA for the faculty and aspiring students to pursue their research and project work. The upgraded facilities in the department include sensors, CVT, Automatic Transmission, etc. The department offers consultancy services in Chassis Design, Engine Design, FEA analysis, and CFD simulations. In the near future, the department aims to start Fuel Testing Laboratory, Advanced Automotive Electronics Laboratory, and a Research center for Alternate Fuels.

The department maintains a close liaison with a number of Universities and Industries in India and abroad through faculty research and collaborative projects.

About Department of Mechanical Engineering

The department was established in 1951 as one of the founding departments of PSG College of Technology.

The department has strong academic and research contributions and maintains collaborations with industries and universities in India and abroad. It has established several specialized laboratories to support teaching, research, and consultancy activities. These include the Festo-PSG Pneumatic Automation Centre, Engineering Design Centre, PSG-Rane Centre for Manufacturing Systems, Machine Tool Research Centre, Wind Tunnel Centre, and the Industry 4.0 Laboratory. Students gain hands-on exposure to modern technologies used in the industry. The Department of Mechanical Engineering offers a wide range of programmes that cater to undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral studies. At the UG level, it provides the B.E. Mechanical Engineering and the unique five-year Mechanical Sandwich programme. The postgraduate programmes include M.E. Engineering Design, M.E. Industrial Engineering, M.E. Computer Integrated Manufacturing, M.E. Energy Engineering, and M.E. Lean Manufacturing, each designed to develop advanced skills in design, manufacturing, automation, operations, and energy systems. The department also offers a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering, enabling scholars to pursue research in emerging and industry-relevant domains.

About the Conference
India, the world's fourth largest economy and the third largest carbon emitter with annual emissions of approximately 2.9 Giga tonnes of CO2 equivalent, maintains a low per capita emission of just 1.8 tons, significantly below the global average. Despite this, India has demonstrated strong environmental leadership by committing to achieve Net Zero emissions by 2070. The Government of India has launched several proactive initiatives targeting emission-intensive sectors like transportation, industries, and power which collectively contribute over 70% of total emissions.
This conference is timely and aligns with the national mission to promote green hydrogen as a clean and sustainable alternative fuel for hard-to-abate sectors such as transport, steel, cement, foundry, and power generation. Recognizing the strategic importance of hydrogen, the Government has allocated 19,744 crores under the National Green Hydrogen Mission for the development of hydrogen production, storage, and distribution infrastructure.

India's renewable energy potential of 1164 GW in wind and 748 GW in solar, offers a robust foundation for green hydrogen production, further advancing the nation's energy independence and sustainability goals. The conference will also explore the role of battery electric vehicles in reducing emissions, with a focus on challenges such as battery materials, lifecycle, fast-charging infrastructure, and end-of-life recycling. By addressing these critical technological, infrastructural, and policy challenges, this conference aims to foster collaboration and innovation that will accelerate India's transition to a low-carbon future and support the nation in achieving its Net Zero vision.

THEMATIC SESSIONS

1. Energy & Decarbonisation Goals and Key Challenges

National goals & barriers

India's energy demand, policy targets, cost of CO₂ abatement, incentives, affordability, adoption barriers and techno-economic challenges.

2. Green Transportation and Propulsion Technologies

EVs, hybrids & hydrogen transport

Electric & hybrid vehicles, long-haul applications, public transport solutions and hydrogen-powered water transport systems.

3. Energy Storage Systems and Technologies

Batteries, flow & system challenges

Fast charging, BESS, lithium/Na/metal-air chemistries, flow batteries, recycling, circular economy and supply-chain constraints.

4. Hydrogen Production Technologies

Green & blue hydrogen pathways

Electrolysis (renewable-powered), biomass routes, solar/wind coupling, green ammonia, techno-economic assessment and enabling factors.

5. Hydrogen Storage Systems

Design, carriers & safety

Modelling and design of storage, materials and sorbents, liquid/solid carriers, safety/handling and fundamentals of hydrogen physics & chemistry.

6. Wind Energy Systems

Resource to repowering

Resource assessment, repowering, grid integration, AI predictive maintenance, wind-to-hydrogen conversion and blade materials/layout optimisation.

7. Solar Energy Systems

PV performance & lifecycle

Perovskite-silicon tandems, module degradation, soiling impacts, recycling & lifecycle management, and AI maintenance for solar farms.

8. Infrastructure and Distribution

Grids & hydrogen logistics

Smart grids, decentralized power systems, hydrogen refuelling infrastructure, and transport & distribution network design.

9. Innovation in Hydrogen Combustion

Combustion systems & LCA

Advanced hydrogen combustion systems, lifecycle assessment, and hydrogen-fuelled heavy-duty trucks and power generation solutions.

10. Industrial and Other Applications

High-temperature & aerospace uses

Hydrogen for high-temperature industrial processes, smart-city integration, system sizing, aerospace applications and fuel-cell deployment strategies.
